**Short answer:** after a kitchen or bathroom renovation, you need more than a wipe of the new tiles. Fine dust travels through the rest of the home, and new surfaces often carry grout haze, silicone and stickers. A [post renovation clean](/services/renovation-cleaning-brisbane/) covers the renovated rooms **and** the dust that moved.

## Why renovation cleans feel different

A new build is empty. A renovation is often lived in. Dust from cutting tiles, sanding plaster and installing joinery settles on soft furnishings, inside wardrobes and on the television in the next room. In character suburbs such as [Albion](/areas/albion/), older timber floors and original tiles sit next to new stone and glass, so the clean has to suit both.

The goal is not only a shiny new kitchen. It is a home you can cook and sleep in without tasting dust.

## What to clean in the renovated rooms

**Kitchen.** Benchtops and splashback free of dust and residue; cupboards and drawers wiped inside, including runners; sink and tapware polished; oven, cooktop and rangehood if they were in the works; appliance stickers and film removed; floor cleaned to suit the surface.

**Bathroom.** Tiles and grout free of haze; shower glass and mirror streak-free; silicone smears removed; vanity inside and out; basin, tapware and toilet cleaned; exhaust grille dusted; floor waste and edges clean.

**Shared dust zones.** Hallways, living rooms and bedrooms next door: skirting, window sills, fans, pendant lights and floors. Cupboards that were left open during the works often need the same inside wipe as the new joinery.

## What not to do

Do not wet-mop construction dust before vacuuming with a fine filter — you will smear a grey paste into the grout and timber. Do not use acidic cleaners on natural stone, terrazzo or polished concrete. Do not scrape coated glass with a worn blade. If the haze is heavy, see our guide on [removing cement haze and paint overspray](/blog/how-to-remove-cement-haze-grout-haze-paint-overspray/) or book [residue removal](/services/paint-plaster-cement-residue-removal-brisbane/).

## How to protect the rest of the home

- Keep doors closed to finished rooms while trades are still cutting and sanding.
- Cover soft furnishings if the dust is still active.
- Empty the renovated cupboards before the clean so every shelf can be wiped.
- Confirm power and water are on for the cleaning day.

## Dust removal first, detail second

On heavy jobs we start with [construction dust removal](/services/construction-dust-removal-brisbane/) from ceiling to floor, then detail the wet areas. That order matters: detail work on a dusty site does not last.

## When to book

Book after the last trade has left — not the day the tiler is still cutting. If you are still living in the home, say so on the quote form so we plan around furniture and family. Send a few photos of the renovated rooms and any rooms that look dusty; we will tell you whether a full home clean or a zone clean is enough.
