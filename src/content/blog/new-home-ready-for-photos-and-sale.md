**Short answer:** sale and listing photos punish dust, haze and smears. Book a [sparkle clean](/services/sparkle-clean-brisbane/) **after** the last trade and **before** photography and styling, so wide shots of glass, floors and wet areas look intentional — not unfinished.

## What buyers see first in photos

- Streaks on stacking doors and balcony glass
- Grey film on dark tiles
- Dust lines on skirting and stair treads
- Fingerprints on gloss joinery
- Cloudy mirrors and shower screens

Apartments and river-side homes in [Newstead](/areas/newstead/) are full of glass. One dull balustrade panel reads as “not ready” in the hero shot.

## Sparkle clean vs a standard final clean

A [final builders clean](/services/builders-final-clean-brisbane/) takes the home to handover standard. A sparkle clean adds the detail pass aimed at presentation: tracks and hinges, cupboard interiors, streak-free glass, polished tapware and a last floor buff. If you are selling or shooting soon after handover, sparkle is usually the right brief.

Owners who are moving in rather than selling may only need a [new home clean](/services/new-home-builders-clean-brisbane/). Match the clean to the goal.

## Timing that works

1. Last trade leaves.
2. Builder’s basic clean (if any) is done.
3. **Sparkle clean** — empty home, full light.
4. Photography.
5. Styling / furniture.
6. First open home — optional light touch-up if trades re-entered.

Do not photograph the day after a wet mop on polished concrete or stone without a proper dry buff. Give floors time to settle and show an even sheen.

## Room priorities for listing day

**Glass and tracks.** Inside glass at minimum; outside where safe and in scope. Tracks vacuumed and wiped.

**Wet areas.** Haze-free tiles, clear screens, dry vanity tops.

**Floors.** Edges and corners detailed; surface cleaned to suit timber, tile or polished concrete.

**Joinery.** Fingerprints off gloss and matte; pantry and robe interiors wiped if camera angles will show open doors.

Walk the home with the [builders clean checklist](/builders-clean-checklist/) the afternoon before the shoot.

## Vacant vs occupied sales

Vacant new builds are simpler: empty rooms, full access, one clean. Occupied or staged homes need protection around furniture and a clear path for the crew. Say which you are on the quote form.
