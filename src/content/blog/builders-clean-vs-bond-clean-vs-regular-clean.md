**Short answer:** a **builders clean** removes construction dust and residue after building or renovation work. A **bond clean** (end-of-lease clean) gets a rental back to the condition in the lease so the tenant gets their bond back. A **regular clean** maintains a home that is already clean. They use different checklists, different products and different amounts of time, so the right one depends on what is in front of you.

## What each clean is for

**Builders clean.** For a home that has just been built or renovated. The problem is construction dust that settles on every surface, inside every cupboard and on every fitting, plus residue: stickers, paint spots, grout haze, silicone, plaster. The goal is handover or move-in standard.

**Bond clean.** For a rental at the end of a tenancy. The problem is everyday grime from living in a home: grease in the oven and rangehood, soap scum, marks on walls, dust in tracks. The goal is to match the property condition report.

**Regular clean.** For a home that is lived in and already clean. The problem is the dust and mess of the past week or fortnight. The goal is upkeep.

## What each includes

| | Builders clean | Bond clean | Regular clean |
|---|---|---|---|
| Construction dust, top to bottom | Yes | No | No |
| Inside cupboards and drawers | Yes | Yes (empty home) | Rarely |
| Stickers, paint, grout haze, silicone | Yes | No | No |
| Oven, rangehood degreased | If in scope | Yes | Sometimes |
| Walls spot-cleaned | Yes | Yes | Rarely |
| Windows and tracks | Yes | Yes | Sometimes |
| New-surface care (stone, polished concrete) | Yes | Not usually | Not usually |
| Photo report | Yes (ours) | Often | No |

The biggest difference is the dust. Construction dust is very fine, it is everywhere at once, and it comes back if it is cleaned in the wrong order. A bond or regular cleaner working with a damp cloth will often smear it rather than remove it.

## Who books each one

- **Builders clean:** builders and site managers before handover; homeowners after handover and before move-in; renovators after the trades leave; owner-builders.
- **Bond clean:** tenants at the end of a lease; sometimes property managers.
- **Regular clean:** homeowners and tenants on a schedule.

## How long each clean takes

Time on site is a good way to see how different these jobs are. A regular clean of a family home might take a couple of hours. A bond clean of the same empty home usually takes most of a day, because ovens, walls and tracks need attention. A builders clean of a new home of the same size often takes a full day for a crew, sometimes longer, because the dust is on every surface at once, inside every cupboard and on every fitting, and residue needs the right product for each surface.

That is also why the prices differ. You are not paying more for the same clean; you are paying for a different, longer job with different equipment.

## What to ask before you book

Whoever you book, ask the same three questions: which rooms and surfaces are included, whether cupboard and drawer interiors are part of it, and what happens if something is missed. A builders cleaner should also be able to tell you how they treat stone, polished concrete and coated glass.

## Which one do you need?

**You have just received the keys to a new build.** A builders clean. Specifically, a [new home clean](/services/new-home-builders-clean-brisbane/) if the builder has done a basic clean, or a full [post construction clean](/services/post-construction-cleaning-brisbane/) if not.

**The renovators have just left your home.** A builders clean, the occupied-home version: our [post renovation cleaning](/services/renovation-cleaning-brisbane/) works around furniture and family. Renovations in established suburbs such as [Chermside](/areas/chermside/) often spread dust well beyond the rooms that were worked on.

**You are moving out of a rental.** A bond clean. Unless the landlord renovated during your lease, a builders cleaner is not the right fit.

**Your home is clean and you want it to stay that way.** A regular clean.

**You are selling a newly built or renovated home.** A [sparkle clean](/services/sparkle-clean-brisbane/), the detailed version of a builders clean, timed for photos and the first open home.

## Why booking the wrong clean costs more

The most common story we hear: an owner books a bond or regular cleaner for a new home because it is cheaper. The cleaner wipes the benchtops and mops the floors, the dust in the cupboards and on the fans stays, the grout haze stays, and the owner books a second clean a week later. Booking the right clean the first time is almost always cheaper.
