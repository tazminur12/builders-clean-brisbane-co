**Short answer:** a basic builder's clean is often a sweep, a mop and a wipe of the obvious surfaces so the home looks presentable for handover. A full [builders final clean](/services/builders-final-clean-brisbane/) goes further — inside cupboards, on fittings, on glass and through residue. If you can still write your name in the pantry dust, the first clean was a different job, not a failed version of the second.

## What a basic builder's clean usually covers

Every builder is different, and your contract is the source of truth. In practice, a basic clean often means:

- Floors swept and mopped
- Benchtops and visible surfaces wiped
- Bathrooms given a once-over
- Rubbish removed from the rooms

It may **not** mean cupboard and drawer interiors, wardrobe shelves, window tracks, ceiling fans, light fittings, sticker and film removal, grout haze treatment or a photo report.

## What owners notice first

The misses that trigger the phone call are almost always the same:

- Dust inside kitchen cupboards and the pantry
- Grey film on dark tiles and shower glass
- Stickers and silicone smears on windows
- Grit in sliding-door tracks
- Dust on blades of ceiling fans and on cornices

In estate homes around [Springfield](/areas/springfield/), dark tiles and large glass make every smear obvious the first morning you walk the home in daylight.

## Why the scopes differ

Builders are handing over a building. Their clean is timed to the inspection and the next job on the program. Owners are preparing a place to live, cook and sleep. Those goals share some surfaces and diverge on detail.

Our guide on [who is responsible for cleaning a new home in Queensland](/blog/who-is-responsible-for-cleaning-new-home-queensland/) explains how contracts usually split the work. The short version: raise cleaning gaps at the inspection, then book the clean you actually need for move-in.

## What a full clean adds

A [new home builders clean](/services/new-home-builders-clean-brisbane/) after handover typically includes:

- Dust removed top to bottom, including fans, lights and cornices
- Windows, frames and tracks
- Stickers and light residue off glass
- Kitchens and bathrooms detailed
- Cupboards and drawers wiped inside
- Floors cleaned to suit the surface
- Supervisor walk against a written checklist
- Photo report when we finish

Compare that list with what you can see in the home. The gap is the scope.

## How to avoid paying twice for the wrong clean

1. Ask the builder, in writing, what the handover clean includes.
2. Walk the home with our [builders clean checklist](/builders-clean-checklist/) in daylight.
3. Book your own clean between handover and move-in, after the last trade has left.
4. Send photos of the worst rooms so the quote matches the real dust load.
