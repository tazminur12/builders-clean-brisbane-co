**Short answer:** before you put a cleaning crew on a Brisbane program, check **insurance, site tickets, written scope, photo reporting and date flexibility**. A cheap hourly rate that skips cupboard interiors and residue will cost you a second clean and a delayed handover.

## The checks that matter on site

**Public liability.** Ask for the certificate and the amount. Keep a copy with the job file.

**Workers compensation.** Confirm cover for the people on your site.

**White cards.** Every person who walks on site should hold a current white card. Ask for copies before day one.

**Police checks.** Required on many residential and apartment programs. Ask early if your principal demands them.

**SWMS.** A safe work method statement for the clean, especially for multi-level and apartment work.

## Scope: the real price comparison

Put both quotes next to the same list:

- Rooms included
- Cupboard and drawer interiors
- Windows, frames and tracks
- Residue (haze, paint, silicone) in or out
- Balconies and entries
- Supervisor check
- Photo report format and timing

If one quote is silent on interiors and residue, it is not the same job. Use our [builders clean checklist](/builders-clean-checklist/) as the shared brief.

## Program fit

Inner-city jobs in places like [Fortitude Valley](/areas/fortitude-valley/) often need lift bookings, loading times and after-hours windows. Ask:

- Can you hold a date and move if trades run late?
- Do you clean apartments in settlement order?
- Who is the single contact on the day?
- How fast is the photo report after sign-off?

A [handover clean](/services/builders-handover-clean-brisbane/) timed to inspection is a different booking from a generic [final clean](/services/builders-final-clean-brisbane/) — say which one you need.

## Photo reports protect everyone

A before-and-after report gives your client proof and gives you a record if something is disputed later. Ask to see a sample report, not only a promise.

## Red flags

- No written scope
- Price that seems too low for the floor area
- No insurance certificate on request
- “We’ll see on the day” instead of a date hold
- No supervisor check or photo proof
