## Why builders clean prices vary so much

Ask three cleaners to quote the same new home and you may get three very different numbers. Usually that is because they are quoting three different jobs. One has priced a sweep and a wipe; one has priced a full final clean with cupboard interiors, tracks and fittings; one has priced by the hour and not said how many hours.

The fix is simple: compare on the same scope. Our [builders clean checklist](/builders-clean-checklist/) gives you a room-by-room list to send to any cleaner.

## The seven price factors

**1. Floor area.** The biggest single factor. We quote from the plans because "four bedrooms" can mean 180 m² or 350 m².

**2. Levels.** Stairs, balustrades, landings and a second floor of wet areas add time.

**3. Wet areas.** Bathrooms, ensuites, powder rooms, laundries and kitchens take the longest. Every extra bathroom adds tiles, grout, glass and tapware.

**4. Glazing.** Floor-to-ceiling glass and stacking doors look beautiful and take time. Tracks full of plaster dust take longer still.

**5. Finishes.** Stone benchtops, polished concrete and dark tiles need slower, surface-specific methods to avoid etching and streaks.

**6. Access.** Apartment buildings with lift bookings, loading docks and long carries add time before the clean starts.

**7. Build stage and residue.** A final clean on a site that had a rough clean is faster than one on a site that did not. Heavy cement haze or paint overspray is quoted separately as [residue removal](/services/paint-plaster-cement-residue-removal-brisbane/).

After-hours or weekend work and travel to outer suburbs can also affect the price. They should be shown as separate lines.

## How to read an itemised quote

A good builders clean quote should tell you:

- **The scope, room by room.** Which rooms, which surfaces, inside cupboards or not, windows inside or out.
- **What is not included.** Carpets, high windows, exterior areas, heavy residue.
- **The date and time on site.** And what happens if the date moves.
- **The total price, with GST shown.** Plus any travel or after-hours charges as separate lines.
- **What happens if something is missed.** A re-clean window for in-scope items.

If a quote does not say these things, ask. A cleaner who cannot tell you what is included cannot be held to it.

## Cheap vs value

The cheapest quote is not always the cheapest outcome. If a cheap clean leaves dust in the cupboards, you clean them yourself or pay for a second clean. If a scraper scratches a pane of glass, the replacement costs more than the clean. If the cleaner does not turn up, a builder misses a handover date.
