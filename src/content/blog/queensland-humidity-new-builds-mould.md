**Short answer:** Queensland’s humidity means new builds can feel damp even when they look finished. Dust holds moisture, wet areas stay wet, and closed-up homes do not dry. A thorough clean and good airflow will not replace fixing a leak — but they are part of stopping early mould before move-in.

## Why new Brisbane homes get damp

- **Construction moisture.** Concrete, plaster, paint and grout release water as they cure.
- **Closed windows.** Homes locked up between trades trap humid air.
- **Dust.** Fine plaster and cement dust holds moisture against surfaces.
- **Wet areas.** Bathrooms and laundries without working exhausts stay wet after cleaning or rain testing.

Growth corridors such as [Logan](/areas/logan/) and the outer estates see the same pattern in storm season: warm days, humid nights, and homes that have not had a chance to breathe.

## What cleaning can and cannot do

**Cleaning can:** remove the dust layer that holds moisture; dry-buff glass and tiles so water spots do not sit; clean floor wastes and silicone edges; leave wet areas dry after the job; give you a clear photo baseline of the surfaces at handover.

**Cleaning cannot:** fix a leaking shower niche, a missing weep hole or a roofing issue. Those are builder or specialist items. If you see active mould, staining that returns after drying, or a musty smell that will not clear, raise it at the [pre-handover inspection](/blog/pre-handover-inspection-checklist-queensland/) and get advice from your builder or a building consultant.

We do not invent mould “guarantees”. We clean to a written checklist and leave the home dry and dust-free so you can see the real condition of the finishes.

## Practical steps before move-in

1. **Open the home.** When it is safe and weather allows, air the rooms after the trades leave.
2. **Run exhausts.** Confirm bathroom and laundry fans work before the clean.
3. **Dry wet trades.** Do not book a sparkle clean the same morning the tiler washes the floor if the rooms are still wet.
4. **Clean top to bottom.** A [handover](/services/builders-handover-clean-brisbane/) or [new home clean](/services/new-home-builders-clean-brisbane/) removes the dust that feeds early growth on sills and in cupboards.
5. **Leave wet areas dry.** After the clean, check showers, sills and the backs of vanity cupboards.
6. **Furniture last.** Soft furnishings in a dusty, damp home lock moisture in. Clean first, then move in.
