**Short answer:** most single homes in Brisbane take **one day on site** for a full builders clean. Larger homes, multi-level builds, heavy residue and apartment access take longer. The quote should state the planned time once we have seen plans or photos.

## Typical time by property type

These are planning guides, not promises. Two homes of the same floor area can differ by hours if one is tidy and the other still has cement haze on every tile.

| Property | Typical time on site |
|---|---|
| Townhouse or small house (≈2–3 bed) | Half day to one day |
| Family home (≈3–4 bed, single storey) | One day |
| Large or two-storey home (≈5+ bed) | One to two days |
| Apartment (per unit) | Half day to one day, plus lift and loading time |
| Commercial fit-out | Quoted from plans; often overnight or staged |

Growth estates such as [North Lakes](/areas/north-lakes/) often run several handovers in the same week. We plan each home separately so one late trade does not push the whole street.

## What slows a builders clean down

**Floor area and levels.** More rooms means more surfaces. Stairs, landings and a second set of bathrooms add time on every level.

**Wet areas.** Kitchens, bathrooms and laundries take the longest: tiles, grout, glass, tapware and cabinetry.

**Glazing.** Floor-to-ceiling glass, stacking doors and dusty tracks are slow, careful work.

**Residue.** Grout haze, paint overspray, plaster and silicone need the right product and more passes. A site that skipped the rough clean almost always costs more time at the final stage.

**Access.** Lift bookings, loading docks, carpark distance and long carries in apartments add time before cleaning starts.

**Power and water.** A crew waiting for a connection is a crew on the clock. Confirm both before the day.

## Rough, final and sparkle: different clocks

A [rough clean](/services/builders-rough-clean-brisbane/) between trades is the shortest stage: debris out, heavy dust lifted, floors clear for the next trade.

A [final builders clean](/services/builders-final-clean-brisbane/) is the full day (or more): every interior surface top to bottom, including cupboard interiors, glass, wet areas and floors.

A [sparkle clean](/services/sparkle-clean-brisbane/) adds detail — tracks, hinges, streak-free glass and a last floor buff — so it sits on top of final-clean time, not instead of it.

Staging saves money because the final crew is not shifting bags of plaster dust before the detail work starts.

## How to plan around handover

1. Book after the last trade has left, not before.
2. Send plans or photos early so the quote includes a realistic day count.
3. Hold a buffer day if the program is tight — trades run late.
4. Ask for the time on site in writing in the quote.

If your date moves, tell us as soon as you know. Flexibility is part of how we work with Brisbane builders and owners.
