# 05 — Service-area page briefs (1 hub, 5 region pages, 15 suburb pages)

**How the area pages are built**
- Every suburb page has **its own** intro, local angle, "what we see here" paragraph and three unique FAQs. Do not copy-paste one suburb's text into another. Google treats near-duplicate suburb pages as thin content.
- Each suburb page also shows the **shared service-area FAQ bank** (below) so every page has 6 FAQs.
- Suburb pages link up to their region page (breadcrumb: Home › Areas › Region › Suburb), across to the 2–3 nearest suburbs, and down to the most relevant service pages ("Services in [Suburb]").
- Region pages list their suburbs and link to every service page.
- **Local detail rule:** the "local angle" lines below describe the *type* of housing and development in each area. Before publishing, the owner or the copywriter checks each line against current local knowledge and removes anything they cannot confirm. Do not add street names, landmarks, counts of homes or school names unless they are verified.
- The primary keyword is always "builders clean [suburb]". Secondary: post construction cleaning [suburb], new home clean [suburb], [suburb] builders cleaners.
- Schema on each area page: `Service` with `areaServed` set to the suburb (as a `City`/`Place`) and `provider` set to the LocalBusiness `@id`.
