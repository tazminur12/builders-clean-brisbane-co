## 5. Pain points (in customers' words where possible)

The biggest fear is a **missed handover date**. The second is **a clean that is not actually clean**. Every money page answers both.

**Builders and site managers**
- Missed dates and no-shows. Reviews of the top competitor praise crews who mobilise even when the builder moves the date at the last minute. Reliability is what builders pay for.
- Slow, small teams. One reviewer said previous cleaners took 3–4 days; a large crew did it in one.
- Re-work and defects at handover. Residue the client finds becomes the builder's problem. They want photo reports and a supervisor walk.
- Damage to premium finishes: scratched glass, etched stone, harsh chemicals on stainless.
- Compliance paperwork. No white card, no SWMS, no insurance certificate means no access to site.
- Chasing the cleaner. They want one contact who answers the phone.
- Price surprises and variations. They want an itemised quote within 24 hours.

**Homeowners and investors**
- "The builder said it was cleaned" but it wasn't: dust in cupboards, paint spots, stickers on glass. Marketplace listings show people booking a second clean after the builder's clean.
- Not knowing whose job it is. Builders usually don't clean unless the contract says so. Official handover guidance does not mention cleaning, so owners are confused.
- No idea what it costs. Marketplace medians for Brisbane after-builders cleans sit roughly in the low hundreds of dollars, while companies avoid publishing prices. (Market reference only, not our pricing.)
- Trust and access. Reviews praise a cleaner who locks up. Investors and sellers need trustworthy access.
- Dust in an occupied home after a renovation, around furniture and kids.
- Poor quality from marketplace cleaners: "had to come back and fix many".
- Deadlines they cannot move: settlement, move-in, photos for sale, tenant start date.

## 7. What competitors do well (we adopt these)

1. Keyword in the title tag and H1 of the homepage (Count On Clean ranks on page 1 with this alone).
2. Builder versus homeowner split on the quote form (Build Clean, Builders Clean).
3. Plan, photo or video upload on the quote form, with a 24-hour itemised quote promise.
4. Three stages explained (rough → final → handover or sparkle) with what each removes.
5. List what changes the price (floor area, levels, glazing, finishes, access, build stage) instead of hiding it.
6. Credentials block: public liability amount, workers compensation, white cards, police checks, SWMS and certificates on request. **Only list what we genuinely hold.**
7. Supervisor walk-through and sign-off, before-and-after photo report, free return for missed in-scope items.
8. Lead-time table: small jobs 48 hours, standard one week, staged projects booked into the builder's program.
9. Queensland-specific FAQ: humidity, mould-prone corners, storm season timing.
10. Named people in reviews. Customers trust a face: put the owner or supervisor's name and photo on the About page.
11. Real suburb list in FAQ and service-area content.
12. Flexibility when trades run late is the most praised trait in every review set. **Make it a headline promise.**
13. Trustworthy keyless access for investors and sellers.
